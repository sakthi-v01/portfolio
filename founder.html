Why

My Co-Founder, Vishnu, and I have always been obsessed with productivity and tools that make us more efficient. We have been exploring these interests since our school days, experimenting with different ideas and figuring things out along the way.

We had a strong inclination towards tech. We were making websites, designing stuff, publishing a book, and constantly being fascinated by what technology could do. Everything we did, in some way, was about being more productive, doing things we actually enjoyed, and creating something that would make people respect us and look up to us.

More than anything, we wanted a way to let our creativity flow without being held back by the tools available to us. Maybe that is where the roots of all this lie: "Wanting nothing to stop our creativity."

He went on to conquer the computer mind, while I went on to explore the human mind. Our paths seemed different, but were they really?

That hunger to create something stayed with us. It did not care about domains or careers. It was simply something we kept seeking. Somewhere along the way, our interests in productivity and in creating something for ourselves and for the world crossed paths.

And thus, Oazis was born.

Oazis is a company built around creating things that, at times, might seem like something you would only see in your dreams. We do not want to simply replicate existing tools. If the existing tools had solved all the problems we saw, perhaps we would have been channeling our creativity into entirely different domains or skills.

But we believe there are still human problems, feelings, and experiences that technology has not fully addressed. These are the problems we see, and these are the problems we want to solve.

We want to improve the way we work. We want to make it easier for people to do what they want to do, without unnecessary barriers holding them back.

"Making people be their best" is, in many ways, the common thread between both our domains.

And through our products, we want to make that happen.

A place where one could come, quench their thirst, relax, and allow their brain to function at its fullest capacity...

Thus...Oazis.
