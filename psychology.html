Why

It started as a personal interest in understanding people and grew into a desire to help them. My areas of interest are productivity, life optimization, discipline, motivation, work-life balance, self-improvement, and development. I try to apply what I study to help people live better, because I believe that change happens in small ways.

One individual, one family, one society, one generation, and eventually the whole world.

I believe everyone's potential is yet to be uncovered, and that people perform best when the right conditions exist: an internal mindset and an external environment.

I want to apply the same thinking on a bigger scale — inside organizations. Helping teams perform at their best while keeping the people in them happy and satisfied is what draws me to roles such as People Operations, Training & Development, and Consultancy.
